User Data Deletion

Effective date: 2 June 2026

1. Your right to erasure

iGiftAid Limited respects your right to request deletion of the personal data we hold about you, in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. This includes any information we receive when you interact with us through Facebook or WhatsApp.

2. Data we may hold from Facebook & WhatsApp

  • Your public profile name and the message content you send us via our Facebook Page or WhatsApp.
  • Your WhatsApp phone number and conversation history with our automated assistant.
  • Any details you voluntarily provide during a conversation (for example, a campaign reference).

3. How to request deletion

Email: igiftaid@gmail.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request”.

Include: the phone number or Facebook name you used to contact us, so we can locate your records.

WhatsApp: you may also reply to any of our messages with the word DELETE to request erasure.

4. What happens next

  • We confirm receipt of your request within 5 working days.
  • We delete your associated personal data within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain certain records (for example, financial records for tax or audit).
  • We notify you by email once deletion is complete.

5. Contact

Questions about data deletion or your rights should be addressed to igiftaid@gmail.com.
