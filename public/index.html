What we do

iGiftAid turns urgent needs into daily gifts. We simplify giving with fair exposure for every campaign, a Daily Needs Wall, and faster, cheaper payouts to the people who need them.

Our mission is simple: a regular and fair flow of donations to the most in-need, every day.

Where our campaigns live

All iGiftAid campaigns are hosted and operated on regulated fundraising platforms; these partners handle donor processing, compliance, and disbursement. iGiftAid coordinates and curates, the platforms below provide the regulated rails.

  • Chuffed.org
  • GoFundMe
