Privacy Policy

I Gift Aid is the trading name of HEART Humanitarian Foundation. This policy is the same one published at hearthumanitarianfoundation.org, because it is the same organisation.

Last updated: 30 August 2026

This policy explains what personal information HEART Humanitarian Foundation collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what you can ask us to do with it.

1. Who we are

HEART Humanitarian Foundation is a nonprofit corporation registered in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania (entity 0015475201) and a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt public charity (EIN 42-2670441). Our registered address is 502 W 7th St, Suite 100, Erie, PA 16502-1333, United States. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

Questions about this policy: Info@HeartHumanitarianFoundation.Org or +1 (717) 881-8186.

2. Information we collect

2.1 If you apply for assistance

  • Your name, and the names and ages of members of your household
  • Your location, living situation and a description of your circumstances
  • Contact details — phone number, WhatsApp number, email address
  • Photographs you choose to send us of your family or your shelter
  • Details needed to transfer funds to you
  • Identity information used to verify that you are who you say you are

Some of this is sensitive. We ask only for what we need to assess an application and to transfer funds lawfully.

2.2 If you donate

Donations are processed by PayPal. We do not receive or store your card or bank details. We receive the information PayPal passes to us, which typically includes your name, email address and the amount, so that we can acknowledge your gift and keep the records the IRS requires of us.

2.3 If you contact us

Your message and the contact details you send it from, including WhatsApp messages you send to our business number.

2.4 If you visit this website

Our web host records standard server log information, including IP address, browser type and pages requested.

This site runs no advertising trackers, no analytics, and no profiling cookies — neither ours nor anyone else's. It is a set of static pages with no embedded map, no translation widget, and no payment or form embeds.

The single outbound request a page makes is to Google Fonts, for the two typefaces the site is set in. Google receives your IP address in order to serve them. Nothing else on this site contacts a third party unless you click a link.

Our sister site, hearthumanitarianfoundation.org, is hosted elsewhere and does load some third-party content; its own privacy policy describes that.

3. Why we use it

  • To assess and decide applications for assistance
  • To verify identity and need, and to screen against sanctions lists as United States law requires of us
  • To create and run fundraising campaigns for approved applicants
  • To transfer funds to beneficiaries and record that we have done so
  • To acknowledge donations and keep the financial records required of a 501(c)(3) charity
  • To answer your questions and support you through the process
  • To meet our legal, tax and reporting obligations

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

5. International transfers

We are based in the United States and our beneficiaries are largely in Gaza and the West Bank, so information necessarily moves between countries, including to and from countries whose data protection laws differ from those where you live. We take reasonable steps to protect it wherever it is held.

6. How long we keep it

We keep records for as long as we need them for the purpose we collected them for, and then for as long as the law requires. In practice:

  • Donation and financial records: at least seven years, to meet IRS recordkeeping and Form 990 reporting requirements.
  • Approved applications and payment records: at least seven years, as part of the charity's financial record.
  • Applications that were not approved: normally no more than two years, unless we are required to keep them longer.
  • General correspondence: normally no more than two years.

7. Your choices and rights

Pennsylvania has no comprehensive consumer privacy statute, and the California Consumer Privacy Act does not generally apply to nonprofit organisations. We are therefore not compelled by those laws to offer the rights below. We offer them anyway, as a matter of policy:

  • Access — ask us what personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to fix information that is wrong
  • Deletion — ask us to erase your information, subject to records we are legally required to keep
  • Withdraw consent — tell us to stop contacting you, or to stop messaging you on WhatsApp, at any time

To exercise any of these, email Info@HeartHumanitarianFoundation.Org. We will respond within thirty days. See our data deletion page for the deletion process specifically, including data associated with Facebook and WhatsApp.

If you are in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area and believe we hold information about you, contact us and we will handle your request in line with the rights those regimes give you.

8. Children

This website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 through it. Applications for assistance necessarily include information about children in an applicant household; that information is provided by the adult applicant and is used only to assess and deliver assistance to that household. If you believe a child has given us information directly, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Cookies and your choices

We set no cookies for advertising, analytics or profiling, and no third party sets any through this site.

Google FontsServes the two typefaces this site uses. Google receives your IP address to deliver them. It sets no cookies here.
Chuffed, GoFundMeOnly reached when you click through to them. Nothing is set until you do, and their own policies apply once you are there.
WhatsAppOnly if you choose to message us. Handled as described in section 4.

Our cookie notice remembers your choice in your own browser's local storage, not in a cookie and not on our servers. To change it, clear this site's data in your browser and the notice will appear again. The site works fully with cookies blocked.

We are a United States charity. Neither federal law nor Pennsylvania law requires a cookie banner of us, and the California Consumer Privacy Act does not generally apply to nonprofit organisations. We show the notice anyway, and we would rather state plainly that there is nothing to consent to than imply otherwise.

10. Security

We limit access to personal information to the people who need it to do this work, use reputable service providers, and serve this site over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information while it is in transit to us.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be noted on the home page.
