3.1 Your account in the secure area

If we approve your application we give you access to a secure area at my.igiftaid.org, where you can see your campaign and ask us to send you a payment. That access is personal to you and to the household named in your application. Do not share your sign-in link or code with anyone. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has it.

We may suspend access where we reasonably believe it is being used by someone other than you, or to obtain money dishonestly.

3.2 Asking to be paid

You decide when to ask for a payment and how much, up to the amount raised and available. We check each request before sending it. A request is not a payment, and we may pause one while we complete checks the law requires of us, or where something about the request does not match what we hold.

Money raised is the charity's property until we pay it out. We will not transfer it to anyone other than the approved applicant or a person you have asked us to pay in writing and we have agreed to.

3.3 ⚠ Payment details are your responsibility, and payments cannot be reversed

Where you ask to be paid in cryptocurrency, you give us the wallet address to send it to. Please read this part carefully.

  • Check the address before you submit it. Compare it character by character with the one in your wallet. Copy and paste it rather than typing it.
  • Check the network. The same address can exist on more than one network. Sending on the wrong one can put the funds beyond reach.
  • A completed transfer cannot be undone. Once a payment is sent it cannot be cancelled, recalled or reversed — not by us, not by the exchange, not by anyone. This is how these networks work.
  • If the address is wrong, the money is gone. If you give us an address that is mistyped, that belongs to someone else, or that you do not control, we cannot recover the payment and we cannot send it again. The money will have left the charity and will not be replaced.

We will send to the address you give us. We cannot check that an address belongs to you, and we do not warrant that it does. If you are not confident about this, ask us before you submit anything — we would far rather answer a question than lose a family's money.

If you would prefer to be paid another way, tell us.

3.4 Keeping us up to date

Tell us if your wallet address, phone number or circumstances change. We act on what you have told us, and an out-of-date detail is the most common way a payment goes astray.
