What we do

I Gift Aid turns urgent needs into daily gifts. We simplify giving with fair exposure for every campaign, a Daily Needs Wall, and faster, cheaper payouts to the people who need them.

Our mission is simple: a regular and fair flow of donations to the most in-need, every day.

Two ways to give

Campaigns

A verified family raising for one large need — treatment, rebuilding, a year of school. Runs for weeks on a regulated fundraising platform.

Browse campaigns →

The Daily Needs Wall

One small, specific thing a family needs this week — usually $18 to $100. Posted once a day, gone in 24 hours. Pick several and settle them together.

See today's wall →

Neither is ranked by popularity. Campaigns are shuffled on every visit and the wall shows whatever expires soonest, so a family who has raised nothing is as visible as one who has nearly finished. That is what fair exposure means here.

Who we are

I Gift Aid is the name we operate under. HEART Humanitarian Foundation is the charity behind it — a nonprofit corporation registered in Pennsylvania and recognised by the Internal Revenue Service as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt public charity. They are one organisation, not two: HEART is doing business as I Gift Aid.

Every donation is made to HEART Humanitarian Foundation. You can verify our status independently with the IRS using our EIN, 42‑2670441, at irs.gov.
