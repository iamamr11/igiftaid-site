The Daily Needs Wall

One small, specific need per family, posted once every 24 hours. Not a campaign, not a target — a single thing someone needs this week, and the amount it costs. Pick as many as you like and settle them together.

Every note expires after 24 hours. A family may post one need a day, so nobody can flood the wall and nothing sits here going stale. If a need is funded it comes down immediately; if it expires unfunded, the family can post it again tomorrow.

We never order this wall by popularity. The default is whatever expires soonest — the most urgent, not the most successful. There is no featured slot and nothing can be paid for.

Nothing matches those filters right now. Try widening them — the wall changes through the day.

Where these come from

Families already verified through our application process can post a need by sending it to us on WhatsApp. Because they are messaging from the number we verified them on, we know who is posting, and the one-a-day limit is enforced against their own record rather than an honour system. Every note is checked before it appears here.

Notes are written by the family and translated where needed. We shorten them for the wall; we do not embellish them.
